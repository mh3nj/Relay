Thoughtful by design

Your practice.
Your pace. Your data.

Relay is designed to keep core learning independent of a Relay-owned application backend. The supplied app stores learning state primarily in your browser, with tools to export and import a backup.

⌂

Browser-first learning

No Relay account is required by the supplied source. Learning data is associated with your browser profile and site origin, not a Relay account. It does not automatically sync across browsers or devices.

↧

Keep a separate backup

Export important progress and keep the backup somewhere safe. Clearing browser data, removing a profile, or losing a device may remove local progress. Treat exported files as personal data.

Optional AI assistance is separate from the core learning workflow. When enabled, relevant prompts or request content are sent to the provider you configure. Review that provider’s policies and never send secrets or sensitive information.
A few useful details

Before you tune in.

No. Translation is one part of a broader learning environment that includes structured lessons, practice modes, games, audio tools, a searchable reference, and progress tracking.

Relay is designed as an installable Progressive Web App with offline-first behavior after its resources have been loaded and cached successfully. Availability can depend on the browser and the deployed build, so test offline reloads on the version you use.

The supplied source is designed to store application state primarily in browser storage and does not require a Relay account or Relay-owned application backend for core learning. Optional AI features send relevant requests to the provider you configure. Hosting, browser, and third-party services have their own data practices.
